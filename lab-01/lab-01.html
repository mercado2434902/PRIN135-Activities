Chocolate Chip Cookies

These homemade chocolate chip cookies are soft, chewy, and easy to make. They're perfect for dessert or an afternoon snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix the butter and sugar until smooth.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract, then stir well.
  4. Mix in the flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop spoonfuls of dough onto a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes or until golden brown.
  8. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.
